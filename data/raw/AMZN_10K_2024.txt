ITEM 1. BUSINESS

General
Amazon seeks to be Earth's most customer-centric company, guided by customer obsession, passion for invention, commitment to operational excellence, and long-term thinking.

Segments
Amazon has organized its operations into three segments: North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Consumers
Amazon serves consumers through online and physical stores, focusing on selection, price, and convenience. Amazon manufactures and sells electronic devices including Kindle, Fire tablet, Fire TV, Echo, Ring, Blink, and eero, and offers Amazon Prime membership.

Developers and Enterprises
Amazon serves developers and enterprises through AWS, offering on-demand technology services including compute, storage, database, analytics, and machine learning.

Human Capital
As of December 31, 2024, Amazon employed approximately 1,556,000 full-time and part-time employees. Over 240,000 Amazon employees around the world have participated in the Career Choice education funding program.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Business and Industry Risks
Amazon's businesses are rapidly evolving and intensely competitive across physical, e-commerce, and omnichannel retail, e-commerce services, web and infrastructure computing services, electronic devices, digital content, advertising, grocery, healthcare, and logistics.

International Operations
Amazon's international sales and operations are subject to risks including local economic and political conditions, government regulation, restrictive governmental actions, foreign currency exchange restrictions, and geopolitical events including war and terrorism. China and India specifically regulate Amazon's businesses through licensing requirements that restrict foreign investment and operation.

Fraudulent Activities of Sellers
Amazon maintains policies to prevent sellers from collecting fraudulent payments or selling unlawful, counterfeit, or stolen goods. Under the A-to-z Guarantee, Amazon may reimburse customers for payments in these situations.

Government Regulation
Amazon faces open investigations based on claims that aspects of its operations infringe competition or consumer protection rules, including aspects of Amazon's fulfillment network, Prime, and AWS's cloud services offering. Amazon is litigating matters alleging price fixing, monopolization, and consumer protection claims brought by state attorneys general and the Federal Trade Commission.

Tax Risk
The Indian tax authority has asserted that tax applies to cloud services fees paid to Amazon in the U.S.; Amazon is contesting this position.
